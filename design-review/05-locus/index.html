Benchmark Construction

Trainable Localization Baseline

Qwen3-VL-4B-Instruct learns the output format through SFT, then optimizes word-position overlap through GRPO.

Adversarial Hallucination Injection

Gemini-3-Flash injects subtle errors into verified clean captions. A text-only detector identifies errors that can be caught without the image. Its feedback guides further injection rounds, retaining errors that require visual verification.

At K = 2, detector recall drops to 0.53 / 0.47 before plateauing at K = 3. Two refinement rounds provide the primary supervision setting.

Supervised and Reinforcement Learning

SFT runs for 40 full-parameter steps. GRPO runs for 100 updates, sampling eight responses per input and comparing their rewards against a frozen SFT reference.

reward = | predicted ∩ reference |
| predicted ∪ reference |

Invalid output: reward 0. Correctly empty prediction and reference: reward 1. The validity gate requires the result envelope and unchanged, complete-word tags.

Training Results

At K = 2, GRPO raises Word F1 from 5.87% to 8.30%. At K = 0 and K = 1, GRPO lowers F1 despite increasing training reward.

For K = 0, the model returns “NO HALLUCINATION” on 970 test captions, including 603 of the 623 captions that contain errors.

All variants share training images and optimization budgets. The 1,000 real-test examples are disjoint from training and validation.
