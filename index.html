Abstract

DetailVerifyBench measures whether multimodal models can pinpoint the words in a long caption that conflict with the image. It includes 1,000 image–caption pairs across five visual domains. Captions average over 200 words and human annotators mark naturally occurring errors in eight categories. We evaluate 15 general-purpose models and train a Qwen3-VL-4B baseline using synthetic examples refined with feedback from a text-only verifier.

Benchmark Construction Pipeline

Gemini-3-Pro drafts captions from selected images. Annotators correct individual visual facts and cross-check the edits. A text diff gives the word boundaries of the original errors.

Adversarial Hallucination Injection Pipeline

Gemini-3-Flash introduces errors into clean captions. GPT-5.2 sees only the resulting text and tries to identify the altered spans. Spans it detects are filtered out, and its feedback guides the next injection round. The interactive demo uses published v1 examples.

Evaluation protocol and metric definitions
Output format
Given a candidate caption and its image, mark erroneous whole words with <HALLUCINATION> tags while preserving the original text. Return NO HALLUCINATION for a verified clean caption.
Word precision, recall and F1
Match predicted and annotated word positions, pooling counts across the test set. Outputs that edit the caption, use malformed tags, or split words count as predicting zero words.
False positive rate (FPR ↓)
Share of valid outputs on clean captions that mark at least one word.
Zero-overlap rate (ZOR ↓)
Share of valid, nonempty predictions on captions with hallucinations that overlap none of the annotated error words.
Recall by error type
Recall on annotated words in each category: Number, Color, Category, Shape, Material, Spatial Relation, OCR, and Other.

Where Models Miss

Tags on the wrong words

GPT-5.4 tags at least one word in 386 erroneous captions with valid outputs. In 214 of those 386, none of its tags overlap an annotated error.

Shared blind spots

Seed2.0-pro, Gemini-3.1-Pro-Preview, and Qwen3.5-397B-A17B all miss 1,165 of 1,710 spans (68.1%) on captions where all three outputs are valid. They jointly miss 128 of 196 directional spans and 167 of 232 single-word color spans.

Incomplete audits

Of the 405 multi-error captions for which Seed2.0-pro returns a valid output, it misses every span in 233, finds some spans in 159, and touches every span in 13. It covers every erroneous word in 11.

Reasoning overrides the image

Qwen3-VL-8B-Thinking reads September as 14 and October as 9 in the chart example. It then accepts the caption’s arithmetic for September and returns NO HALLUCINATION.

Trainable Localization Baseline

We fine-tune Qwen3-VL-4B-Instruct on 1,345 synthetic training images with 150 validation examples. Both sets are disjoint from the real test set. Each setting uses 40 supervised fine-tuning (SFT) steps and 100 Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) updates.

  1. Refine the injected errorsUse up to K feedback rounds to remove errors the detector can find from text alone. K = 0 is the unfiltered initial injection.
  2. Teach the tagging formatSFT trains the model to return the original caption with tags on erroneous words, or an explicit clean result.
  3. Optimize word overlapGRPO assigns zero to invalid outputs, one to correct clean outputs, and word-position intersection over union to other valid outputs.

Adversarial injection ablations

Lower detector scores indicate errors that are harder to infer from the caption alone. At K = 2, detector recall drops to 53% for GPT-5.2 and 47% for Qwen3-VL-8B-Thinking, compared with 75% and 71% before filtering. A third round changes recall to 52% and 47%.
